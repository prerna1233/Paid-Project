## 📋 Feature Overview

### 1. ✍️ Write a Blog
**Button:** "Write a Blog" (top of page)

**Steps:**
1. Click "Write a Blog" button
2. Enter title
3. (Optional) Upload image
4. Enter description/content
5. Click "Publish Blog"

**Result:** Blog appears immediately at the top of the grid

---

### 2. 👤 View Your Blogs
**Button:** "View Your Blogs (X)" (top of page)

**Steps:**
1. Click "View Your Blogs" button
2. Modal opens showing only your blogs
3. Click any blog card to view details
4. Click delete button to remove a blog

**Result:** See all blogs you created in a dedicated modal

---

### 3. ❤️ Like a Blog
**Button:** Heart icon on each blog card

**Steps:**
1. Click the heart icon on any blog
2. Heart turns red, like count increases
3. Click again to unlike

**Result:** Toggle like status with real-time counter updates

---

### 4. 💬 Comment on a Blog
**Location:** In blog detail modal (Comments section)

**Steps:**
1. Click "Read More" on any blog
2. Scroll to Comments section
3. Type comment in input field
4. Press Enter or click "Post"

**Result:** Comment appears immediately with your name and timestamp

---

### 5. 🗑️ Delete Your Blog
**Button:** Delete button in "Your Blogs" modal

**Steps:**
1. Open "View Your Blogs"
2. Click "✕ Delete" on a blog
3. Confirm deletion

**Result:** Blog removed from all views

---

### 6. 📖 Read Full Details
**Button:** "Read More" or click blog title

**Steps:**
1. Click "Read More" button or blog title
2. Modal opens with full content
3. Like, comment, or just read
4. Close with X button

**Result:** View complete blog with all interactions available
