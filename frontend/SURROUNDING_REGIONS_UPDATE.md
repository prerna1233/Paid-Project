# Surrounding Regions Update - Kishanganj District

## ✅ Updated Section: "Surrounding Regions & Nearby Attractions"

### **Geographical Context**
Kishanganj district is located at a strategic tri-junction where:
- **East**: West Bengal (Interstate border)
- **North**: Nepal (International border)
- **West**: Purnia District, Bihar
- **Southwest**: Araria District, Bihar

---

## **4 Surrounding Regions Cards**

### **1. West Bengal (Eastern Border) 🇮🇳**

**Location**: Shares eastern border with Kishanganj

**Key Attractions**:
- ✈️ Siliguri - Major City (65 km east)
- 🌳 Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary - Biodiversity hotspot
- 🍃 North Bengal Tea Gardens - Scenic plantations
- ⛰️ Darjeeling Hills - Hill station access point
- 🚂 New Jalpaiguri Railway - Major connectivity hub

**Travel Advisory**: Interstate travel - Valid photo ID required

---

### **2. Nepal (Northern Border) 🇳🇵**

**Location**: International border - Northern side of Kishanganj

**Key Attractions**:
- 🏛️ Kakarbhitta Border Town - Entry point (45 km north)
- 🍵 Ilam District - Famous tea gardens region
- ⛰️ Eastern Nepal Hill Stations - Trekking routes
- 🌳 Mechi Zone - Natural landscapes & culture
- 📍 Birtamode - Commercial hub near border

**Travel Advisory**: International border - Passport or voter ID required (ALERT - Red background)

---

### **3. Purnia District (Western Border) 🇮🇳**

**Location**: Adjacent Bihar district - Western side of Kishanganj

**Key Attractions**:
- 🏛️ Purnia City - Divisional headquarters (80 km west)
- 🙏 Mata Puran Devi Temple - Religious significance
- 📍 Kali Mandir Gulabbagh - Historical temple
- 🚜 Agricultural Markets - Regional trade center
- 🏰 Jalalgarh Fort Ruins - Historical heritage

**Travel Advisory**: Within Bihar - No special permits required

---

### **4. Araria District (Southwestern Border) 🇮🇳**

**Location**: Adjacent Bihar district - Southwest of Kishanganj

**Key Attractions**:
- 🏛️ Araria Town - District center (50 km southwest)
- 🙏 Kali Asthan Temple - Major pilgrimage site
- 🌊 Kosi River Basin - Natural water system
- 🌾 Rice & Maize Fields - Agricultural landscape
- 📍 Forbesganj - Cultural & commercial town

**Travel Advisory**: Within Bihar - No special permits required
